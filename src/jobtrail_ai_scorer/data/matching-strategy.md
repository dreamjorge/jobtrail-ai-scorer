# Public matching strategy

## Target roles
Backend, platform, and developer tooling engineering roles.

## Required signals
Production software ownership, service design, testing, and operational responsibility.

## Equivalent skills
Comparable experience in Python, Go, Java, or other strongly typed or production scripting ecosystems.

## Adjacent roles
Site reliability, infrastructure, data platform, and developer experience roles.

## Exclusion rules
Exclude roles requiring unrelated domain certifications, mandatory relocation, or unavailable work authorization.

## Location and seniority tolerance
Prefer remote-compatible roles and consider adjacent seniority when responsibilities and growth are credible.

## Evidence policy
Use only the job listing and this public ruleset. Treat missing evidence as unknown rather than inferred.
